# Modification Notice — Super Limeade Factory

Super Limeade Factory is a modified Android edition of the GPLv3-released OUYA source of **Super Lemonade Factory**.

## Who changed it

Android restoration, modernization, release-hardening, branding, and packaging changes were made in **2026** by **Ramy Baheeg**.

## Relationship to the original game

- Original game: **Super Lemonade Factory**
- Original developer: **Shane Brouwer / Initials Video Games**
- This Android edition: **Super Limeade Factory**
- Publisher of this Android edition: **Ramy Baheeg**
- Status: independent restoration/modification
- Affiliation: **not produced, sponsored, or endorsed by Shane Brouwer or Initials Video Games**

No response or silence from the original developer is represented as approval or endorsement.

## Material modification areas

The modified edition includes, among other release-history changes:

- current HARMAN AIR / Android packaging and API-level modernization;
- modern Android input, lifecycle, display, foldable, and touch-control compatibility work;
- adaptive touch-control placement and persistent control-height preference;
- corrected customer-facing control instructions and repaired Android runtime behavior;
- privacy, source-disclosure, license, and third-party notice surfaces;
- Super Limeade Factory naming and lime-green visual treatment for the independent Android edition;
- release verification, manifest inspection, source provenance, and fail-closed production-signing tooling.

Repository history is the authoritative detailed record of file-level changes.
