# Privacy Policy — Super Limeade Factory

**Developer / publisher:** Ramy Baheeg  
**Privacy contact:** ramy.baheeg@gmail.com  
**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

Super Limeade Factory does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The game does not require an account and does not use advertising or analytics services.

## Data stored on the device

Game progress is stored only on the user's device in Android app-private local storage. Ramy Baheeg does not receive or have access to that locally stored progress.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress.

## Network and third-party data sharing

The game does not require an internet connection for normal play. It does not transmit game progress or personal or sensitive user data to Ramy Baheeg or to third parties.

## Data security

Because the app does not transmit personal or sensitive user data to a server, there is no server-side personal-data processing by the developer. Locally stored game progress remains in Android app-private storage under the protections provided by the user's device and operating system.

## Data retention and deletion

The developer retains no server-side user data for this app. There is therefore no remote user-data retention or deletion process.

Users can remove locally stored game progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Privacy inquiries

Questions about this privacy policy may be sent to **ramy.baheeg@gmail.com**.
