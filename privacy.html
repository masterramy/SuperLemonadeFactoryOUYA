Privacy Policy — Super Lemonade Factory Port

Developer: Ramy Baheeg
Privacy contact: ramy.baheeg@gmail.com
Effective date: September 4, 2026

Super Lemonade Factory Port does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The game does not require an account and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Game progress is stored only on the user's device in the app's private local storage. Ramy Baheeg does not receive or have access to that locally stored progress. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress.

The game does not require an internet connection for normal play. No server-side user data is retained by the app, so there is no remote user-data deletion process.

Privacy inquiries may be sent to ramy.baheeg@gmail.com.

About this Android port

Super Lemonade Factory Port is an independent Android port of Shane Brouwer / Initials' Super Lemonade Factory, built from the GPLv3-released OUYA source. This Android release is published by Ramy Baheeg and is not presented as an official Initials publication or endorsement.

Corresponding source for this Android port is maintained at github.com/masterramy/SuperLemonadeFactoryOUYA.
